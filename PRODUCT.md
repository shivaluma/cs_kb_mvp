# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Customer Support agents, CS Ops reviewers, QA/owner teams, and frontend/backend maintainers use this internal SOP Knowledge Base while answering customer issues, reviewing SOP changes, governing structured knowledge, and validating grounded AI behavior. Most primary users are non-technical CS users who need to search, open, and verify SOP guidance quickly without learning chunk, embedding, or pipeline internals.

## Product Purpose

CS SOP Knowledge Base helps support teams search governed SOPs, open the full source context, inspect exact highlighted source references, manage document review and extraction quality, and ask grounded AI questions with citations. Success means a CS user can find the right SOP, understand why it matched, verify the answer from cited sources, and escalate uncertainty or conflicts without feeling lost.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exacting, and operational. The product should feel like a trusted CS operations workbench: focused, accountable, and quietly helpful rather than promotional or technically showy.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing-page composition, dashboard noise, decorative cards, raw technical metadata in normal CS flows, exposed chunk IDs or vector scores, overwhelming filter walls, AI answers detached from sources, and interfaces that make users guess whether SOP guidance is approved or uncertain.

## Design Principles

- Search is the primary interaction; everything else should support finding and verifying the right SOP.
- Full SOP context is the source of truth; chunks and extraction artifacts are review/debug details, not the normal reading model.
- Ground every AI answer and every review action in visible citations, source refs, or pipeline evidence.
- Separate normal CS workflows from admin/debug workflows so governance power does not pollute day-to-day lookup.
- Prefer progressive disclosure: show the next useful action, keep advanced metadata available but tucked behind review/admin surfaces.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Design for laptop-first internal operations with responsive layouts for common smaller screens. Keep labels plain, contrast strong, controls keyboard reachable, status changes explicit, and source/citation relationships visible without relying on color alone. Avoid motion or decoration that distracts from high-stakes SOP verification.
